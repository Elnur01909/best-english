# 02_unit1A_theory.md

```yaml
unit:
  id: UNIT_1A
  title: The Legal Profession
  level: Foundation

sections:

  - id: WORKING_IN_LAW

    concepts:

      lawyer:
        definition: >
          General term used for a person qualified to practise law.

      solicitor:
        definition: >
          Lawyer who advises clients, drafts legal documents,
          represents clients and often works in a law firm.

      barrister:
        definition: >
          Specialist advocate who provides legal opinions and
          represents clients in court.

      attorney:
        definition: >
          American term for lawyer.

      judge:
        definition: >
          Person appointed to hear cases and make legal decisions.

    england_and_wales_system:

      legal_professions:
        - solicitor
        - barrister

      solicitor_route:
        - university studies
        - legal practice course
        - training contract
        - qualification as solicitor

      barrister_route:
        - legal studies
        - Bar examinations
        - qualification as barrister

      judge_route:
        - become experienced lawyer
        - apply for judicial appointment
        - selection process

    solicitor_characteristics:

      employment:
        - law firms
        - partnerships
        - legal practices

      responsibilities:
        - advise clients
        - draft documents
        - represent clients
        - conduct litigation
        - specialise in legal fields

      terminology:
        acting_for_client: >
          Representing a client.

        litigation: >
          Process of making a claim in the civil court.

    barrister_characteristics:

      employment:
        - self-employed
        - chambers

      responsibilities:
        - advocacy
        - plead cases
        - legal opinions
        - court representation

      terminology:

        advocacy:
          definition: >
            Skill of speaking on behalf of someone in court.

        pleading_a_case:
          definition: >
            Presenting a client's case before a court.

        right_of_audience:
          definition: >
            Legal right to appear before a court and be heard.

    usa_system:

      lawyer_title:
        attorney

      notes:
        - No solicitor/barrister distinction.
        - Attorney is general professional title.

  - id: CIVIL_AND_CRIMINAL_LAW

    overview:

      criminal_law:
        definition: >
          Area of law dealing with offences against persons,
          property and society.

        characteristics:
          - state prosecutes offender
          - public interest focus

      civil_law:
        definition: >
          Area of law dealing with rights and obligations of
          individuals and companies.

        characteristics:
          - disputes between parties
          - compensation-focused

    terminology:

      matter:
        definition: >
          Legal subject or legal issue.

      will:
        definition: >
          Legal document explaining what happens to property
          after death.

      steal:
        definition: >
          Take property belonging to another person with the
          intention of keeping it.

  - id: CIVIL_COURT_CLAIMS

    core_concept:

      claim:
        definition: >
          Civil court action started against another party.

    terminology:

      claimant:
        definition: >
          Person bringing the claim.

      plaintiff:
        definition: >
          Traditional term for claimant.
          Still commonly used in the USA.

      defendant:
        definition: >
          Person defending the claim.

      proceedings:
        definition: >
          Formal legal process before a court.

      claim_form:
        definition: >
          Document used to start legal action.

      court_fee:
        definition: >
          Payment required to issue proceedings.

      legal_costs:
        definition: >
          Court fees and lawyer fees.

      dispute:
        definition: >
          Serious disagreement between parties.

      procedure:
        definition: >
          Established legal process.

    civil_claim_process:

      step_1:
        issue_claim

      step_2:
        serve_claim

      step_3:
        defendant_response

      step_4:
        court_hearing

      step_5:
        judgment

      step_6:
        order_enforcement

    legal_actions:

      issue_claim:
        meaning: >
          Start proceedings in civil court.

      serve_claim:
        meaning: >
          Officially deliver claim documents to defendant.

      respond_to_claim:
        meaning: >
          Confirm receipt and explain intended response.

      hear_case:
        meaning: >
          Court listens to parties and evidence.

      find_in_favour:
        meaning: >
          Decide that one party wins.

      give_judgment:
        meaning: >
          Officially announce decision.

      make_order:
        meaning: >
          Officially require a party to do something.

      bailiff:
        meaning: >
          Officer who can enforce court orders and
          recover property or money.

    county_court:

      jurisdiction:
        Most civil claims.

      notes:
        - More than 200 County Courts.
        - Found throughout England and Wales.

    small_claims:

      maximum_value:
        "£5000"

      note:
        Small claims are handled under a special procedure,
        not in a separate court building.

  - id: LAW_OF_TORT

    definition:

      tort:
        definition: >
          Civil wrong causing harm or loss.

    key_principles:

      duty_of_care:
        definition: >
          Legal obligation to act carefully and avoid
          causing harm.

      breach:
        definition: >
          Violation of legal duty.

      negligence:
        definition: >
          Failure to exercise reasonable care.

      carelessness:
        definition: >
          Lack of sufficient care causing injury or damage.

    injured_person:
      definition: >
        Person harmed by another's conduct.

    damages:
      definition: >
        Financial compensation awarded by court.

    compensation:
      definition: >
        Money paid for injury or loss.

    sue:
      definition: >
        Start legal proceedings against someone.

    allegation:
      definition: >
        Statement claiming something happened
        but not yet proven.

    grounds:
      definition: >
        Legal reasons supporting a claim.

    no_win_no_fee:
      definition: >
        Arrangement where client pays lawyer only
        if case succeeds.

    examples_of_torts:

      - dangerous_shop_floor
      - false_statement_about_person
      - false_newspaper_story
      - excessive_noise_affecting_neighbours

  - id: AREAS_OF_LAW

    contract_law:

    company_law:
      focus: Companies and corporate structures

    land_law:
      focus: Ownership and transfer of property

    law_of_tort:
      focus: Civil wrongs

    equity_and_trusts:
      focus: Trusts and fiduciary arrangements

    employment_law:
      focus: Employment relationships

    family_law:
      focus: Marriage, divorce, children

    immigration_law:
      focus: Entry and residence rights

    intellectual_property_law:
      focus: Copyright, patents, trademarks

    criminal_law:
      focus: Crimes and punishment

  - id: EMPLOYMENT_LAW_TERMS

    unfair_dismissal:
      definition: >
        Dismissal without valid legal reason.

    redundancy:
      definition: >
        Job loss because position is no longer needed.

    maternity_leave:
      definition: >
        Leave connected with childbirth.

    sick_pay:
      definition: >
        Payment during illness absence.

    discrimination:
      definition: >
        Unequal treatment based on protected characteristics.

  - id: BUSINESS_LAW_TERMS

    capital:
      definition: >
        Money and assets invested in business.

    formation_of_business:
      definition: >
        Creation of new business entity.

    partnership:
      definition: >
        Business owned by two or more persons.

    merger:
      definition: >
        Combination of businesses into one entity.

    insolvency:
      definition: >
        Inability to pay debts.

  - id: LAND_LAW_TERMS

    landlord:
      definition: >
        Owner renting property.

    tenant:
      definition: >
        Person paying rent to occupy property.

    lease:
      definition: >
        Right to occupy property for fixed period.

    conveyancing:
      definition: >
        Legal transfer of property ownership.

    real_estate:
      definition: >
        Land and buildings.
```
