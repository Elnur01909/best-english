## QUIZ 1 — Contract for Services (Termination Clause)

### Contract Extract
> 15.1 Either party may terminate the Agreement immediately by written notice in the event that the other party commits a **(A) breach** of the Agreement which the party serving the notice reasonably considers is not capable of **(B) remedy** or the other party has continued in any breach of the Agreement for more than 21 days after being given notice in writing of such breach. The Company may terminate the Agreement **(C) forthwith** by written notice to the Customer **(D) in the event that** the Customer is a **(E) company**, and the company passes a **(F) resolution** or the court makes an order that it should be **(G) wound up** or that an administrator be appointed, or if the Customer makes an arrangement with its **(H) creditors**, or if the Customer being an individual at any time becomes **(I) bankrupt**, or the Customer is a **(J) partnership** and any partner becomes bankrupt.

### Match the Terms

| Letter | Term | Definition |
|--------|------|------------|
| A | breach | a failure to carry out obligations |
| B | remedy | a legal solution which compensates for a failure to carry out obligations |
| C | forthwith | immediately |
| D | in the event that | if |
| E | company | an incorporated business which directors run and shareholders own |
| F | resolution | a formal decision made at a meeting |
| G | wound up | brought to an end; permanently stopped |
| H | creditors | people or organisations who are waiting for payment |
| I | bankrupt | not having enough money to pay his or her debts |
| J | partnership | a business relationship in which people have joint and several liability |

### Quiz Questions (Multiple Choice format)
**Q1.** "The Company may terminate the Agreement _______ by written notice." What does the missing word mean?
- A) eventually  
- B) immediately ✓  
- C) conditionally  
- D) partially  

**Q2.** "The customer makes an arrangement with its _______." The missing word refers to:
- A) shareholders  
- B) employees  
- C) people or organisations waiting for payment ✓  
- D) directors  

**Q3.** A formal decision made at a meeting is called a:
- A) resolution ✓  
- B) revision  
- C) remedy  
- D) ruling

The **(G) signatory** on the application form shall be **(H) deemed** to be the Customer. The Company will not enter into a supply agreement with **(I) tenants** of furnished premises or tenants on a short-term **(J) lease** (of three months or less).

### Match the Terms

| Letter | Term | Definition |
|--------|------|------------|
| A | set out | written, explained |
| B | charges | money that you must pay in return for receiving something |
| C | liability | legal responsibility |
| D | joint | together, as a group of people |
| E | several | individual |
| F | premises | the buildings and land that a business uses |
| G | signatory | the person who signs a document |
| H | deemed | considered |
| I | tenants | people who pay rent to the owner of a building or land |
| J | lease | a type of rental agreement |

### Quiz Questions
**Q4.** "Liability shall be joint and several." 'Several' in this legal context means:
- A) many  
- B) individual ✓  
- C) serious  
- D) separate  

**Q5.** "The signatory on the form shall be deemed to be the Customer." 'Deemed' means:
- A) required  
- B) considered ✓  
- C) expected  
- D) nominated  

**Q6.** 'Premises' in a contract refers to:
- A) introductory statements  
- B) promises made by both parties  
- C) the buildings and land a business uses ✓

## QUIZ 3 — Contract for Sale of Goods

### Contract Extract
> The Seller **(B) retains (C) title** to the Goods **(D) notwithstanding** delivery to the Buyer until the Seller has been paid in full. The Buyer shall **(F) notify** the Seller within a reasonable time if the Goods are damaged. If the Buyer fails to do this he is **(G) deemed** to have accepted the Goods. The Seller will **(J) refund** the cost of postage if the Goods are in fact defective.

### Match the Terms

| Letter | Term | Definition |
|--------|------|------------|
| A | premises | the buildings and land that a business uses |
| B | retains | keeps |
| C | title | the legal right to have ownership of something |
| D | notwithstanding | despite, in spite of |
| E | such | already mentioned |
| F | notify | tell |
| G | deemed | considered |
| H | defect | fault, imperfection |
| I | enable | to make possible, to allow |
| J | refund | to give someone his or her money back |

### Quiz Questions
**Q7.** "The Seller retains title to the Goods notwithstanding delivery." 'Notwithstanding' means:
- A) after  
- B) before  
- C) despite ✓  
- D) because of  

**Q8.** "The Seller retains title to the Goods." 'Title' in this context means:
- A) the name of the contract  
- B) the legal right to ownership ✓  
- C) a heading in the document  
- D) the delivery note  

**Q9.** "The Goods are damaged or have a defect." A 'defect' is:
- A) a delay in delivery  
- B) a missing document  
- C) a fault or imperfection ✓  
- D) an overcharge

## QUIZ 4 — Contract for the Hire of a Bicycle

### Contract Extract
> The Renter **(C) undertakes** not to misuse or damage the Bicycle, ordinary **(D) wear and tear** excepted. The period of hire **(E) commences** at collection. The Owner shall not be liable for any damage **(G) whatsoever**. The Renter shall **(I) indemnify** the Owner in respect of all costs and claims which may arise out of use of the Bicycle and which the Owner may **(J) incur**.

### Match the Terms

| Letter | Term | Definition |
|--------|------|------------|
| A | part with possession | to be separated from something |
| B | thereof | of it (of the bicycle) |
| C | undertakes | promises or agrees to do something |
| D | ordinary wear and tear | the usual amount of damage from normal use |
| E | commences | starts |
| F | aforesaid | already mentioned |
| G | whatsoever | at all, of any type |
| H | defects | faults in something; not perfect |
| I | indemnify | to promise to pay someone for any loss they suffer |
| J | incur | to be obliged to spend money on something |

### Quiz Questions
**Q10.** "The Renter undertakes not to misuse the Bicycle." 'Undertakes' means:
- A) agrees or promises ✓  
- B) attempts  
- C) is required  
- D) has the right  

**Q11.** "The Owner shall not be liable for any damage whatsoever." 'Whatsoever' means:
- A) to any extent  
- B) of any type, at all ✓  
- C) in writing  
- D) beyond the contract  

**Q12.** "The Renter shall indemnify the Owner in respect of all costs." To 'indemnify' means:
- A) to confirm payment  
- B) to insure against  
- C) to promise to pay for any loss suffered ✓  
- D) to guarantee delivery
