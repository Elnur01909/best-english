## Key Question 3 — Which ICSID / UNCITRAL / ICC / LCIA cases involving Azerbaijan or Azerbaijani entities were registered, decided or in enforcement in 2025–2026, and who are the claimants/counsel?

### Takeaway
Four live or recently decided matters dominate: Libra LLC & others / Estate of Efruz Muduroglu (UK) v. Azerbaijan (ICSID ARB/23/46, pending, construction/real estate); Leshkasheli (Georgia) & Rosserlane Consultants (UK) v. Azerbaijan (ICSID ARB/20/20, US$700m claim declined 21 Mar 2025, partial annulment pending); Bahari (Iran) v. Azerbaijan (PCA 2022-49, UNCITRAL, ~US$800m claim dismissed 21 Sep 2026 with costs); and Palmali/Mansimov (Türkiye) v. SOCAR (LMAA award Feb 2026 for SOCAR; English High Court enforcement judgment 7 Aug 2026). Counsel names were not visible in the snippets except tribunal members.
