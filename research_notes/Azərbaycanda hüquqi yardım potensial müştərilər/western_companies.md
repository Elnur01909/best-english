## Key Question 1 — Which Western SMEs, startups, scale-ups, consultancies, education providers, fintechs, renewable developers, logistics firms and contractors announced Azerbaijan market entry or new projects in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
Fourteen named Western legal entities have dated 2025–2026 evidence of entering or expanding in Azerbaijan; the strongest "needs local English-language counsel now" profiles are the SMEs and first-time entrants: Vulcan Completion Products (UK), GUGLER Water Turbines (Austria), ENNA Group (Croatia), Wonderful (Netherlands), Gran Tierra Energy (Canada), Cipriani (Italy/Luxembourg), Nobu Hospitality (US) and the CSG defence JVs (Czech Republic). Exploratory-stage entrants (Enercon, Zhero, Xlinks) and large groups with existing Baku presence (VNG, Carlsberg, Squire Patton Boggs) are lower priority.

### Inferences
- The best-fit clients for an English-language Azerbaijani-law practice are the SME/first-entrant profiles (Leads 1–4, 6–8): each has a concrete, dated trigger (new office, signed contract, MoU with a ministry, PSA ratification, brand agreement) and no evidence of an existing Baku legal function.

- The Green Energy Corridor forum (22 Oct 2026) is generating a pipeline of European interconnector/renewables developers (Zhero, Xlinks, ENNA) still at MoU/meeting stage — good 6–12-month prospects.
- German SME interest is being funnelled through AHK Azerbaijan events; a German business mission to Azerbaijan is planned for December 2026 (see KQ3), which should surface additional first-time entrants.
