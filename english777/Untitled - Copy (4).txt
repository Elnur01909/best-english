# ==========================================
# COLLOCATIONS
# ==========================================

collocations:

  definition: >
    Collocations are words that naturally occur together.
    Accurate legal English depends heavily on correct collocation usage.

  examples:

    good:

      - draft a contract
      - honour a contract
      - breach a contract
      - qualify as a lawyer
      - act for a client
      - gain experience
      - issue a claim
      - sit as a judge
      - take an exam
      - suffer a loss
      - set up a business
      - run a business
      - claim damages

    bad:

      - write a contract
      - respect a contract
      - infringe a contract

  principle:
    Some nouns collocate with many verbs.
    Some nouns collocate with only one verb.
    Accurate legal communication requires memorisation
    of standard legal collocations.
